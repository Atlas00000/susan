## SURVEY QUESTIONS AND FLOW

### Survey Purpose
The **Scent Discovery Survey** is designed to match customers with their perfect fragrance by understanding their preferences, lifestyle, personality, and scent intensity preferences. The survey uses a weighted scoring system across four scent families (oud, amber, floral, gourmand) to generate personalized recommendations.

### Survey Experience
- **Total Questions:** 5
- **Estimated Time:** 2-3 minutes
- **Format:** Single choice per question
- **Visual Design:** Luxury-focused with animations and progress tracking
- **Result:** AI-generated personalized scent profile with top 3 product matches

## Scoring Algorithm

### Weight Calculation System

**Each answer contributes weights across 4 scent families:**
- **Oud** - Rich, complex, traditional luxury
- **Amber** - Warm, elegant, timeless
- **Floral** - Fresh, beautiful, versatile
- **Gourmand** - Sweet, bold, unconventional

**Total Score Calculation:**
1. Multiply each question's weights by 1.0 (answer weight)
2. Sum all weights per scent family across 5 questions
3. Calculate total score for each scent family
4. Normalize scores to percentages
5. Match products based on highest family scores

**Product Matching Logic:**
1. Products are tagged with primary and secondary scent families
2. Match products where primary family matches user's top scores
3. Rank by alignment strength
4. Return top 3 matches with reasoning
